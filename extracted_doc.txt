1. Programme Overview
The HIPS Global Experience 2026 is a curated international professional development and immersion programme designed for professionals, executives, entrepreneurs and organisational teams seeking to broaden their professional perspective through learning, business exposure, networking and cultural experience.
The Kenya × Rwanda experience combines structured professional engagement with guided destination experiences across Nairobi and Kigali. It is designed to move beyond a conventional conference or a conventional leisure trip by bringing professional learning and international exposure into one coherent experience.
The central idea is simple: participants should learn beyond the classroom, experience environments beyond their usual context, build meaningful connections and return with perspectives they can apply to their professional lives.
2. Theme & Intellectual Focus
IMPACT THAT SCALES: BEYOND BORDERS
The experience extends the HIPS 4.0 intellectual foundation, where “Impact That Scales” was built around Execution Intelligence, Systems Thinking, Influence at Scale, and Career Capital & Leverage. The international experience carries these ideas beyond the traditional training room and into a different professional, business and cultural environment.
The guiding question for the experience is: How do capable professionals turn knowledge, ideas and opportunity into measurable results that can scale—across roles, organisations, industries and borders?

2. THE PROFESSIONAL FOCUS
IMPACT THAT SCALES
The intellectual foundation of HIPS 4.0 is built around the question:
How do capable professionals turn knowledge, ideas and opportunity into measurable results that can scale?
The experience builds on four interconnected areas:
01 — EXECUTION INTELLIGENCE: Turning knowledge, plans and intentions into measurable results. Participants examine the gap between knowing and delivering, and the practical systems required to consistently execute.
02 — SYSTEMS THINKING :Moving from individual effort to sustainable ways of working. Participants explore how professionals and organisations create systems that allow performance and impact to continue beyond individual effort.
03 — INFLUENCE AT SCALE: Creating influence beyond formal position.
The focus is on professional credibility, collaboration, communication, stakeholder relationships and the ability to move people and ideas.
04 — CAREER CAPITAL & LEVERAGE: From Connections to Career Capital A curated networking experience designed to help participants identify the relationships they need, engage strategically and turn meaningful connections into opportunities, influence and career leverage.
THE JOURNEY
EXECUTE → BUILD → INFLUENCE → LEVERAGE → SCALE

3. WHY KENYA × RWANDA?
The two destinations provide different but complementary dimensions to the experience.
KENYA — BUSINESS, CONNECTION & EXPERIENCE
Nairobi provides the business and metropolitan component of the programme, alongside opportunities for professional engagement, networking, sightseeing and leisure.
RWANDA — PROFESSIONAL EXPOSURE, CULTURE & EXPERIENCE
Kigali provides the second dimension of the experience, combining professional engagement with cultural exposure, city exploration, art, adventure and reflection.
The objective is not simply to visit two countries, but to give participants an opportunity to experience different environments, interact with people and places outside their normal professional context, and broaden their perspective.
